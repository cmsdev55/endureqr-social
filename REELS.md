## Day 2: How big should a QR code be?

- **When:** Day 2
- **Video:** https://cmsdev55.github.io/endureqr-social/media/d02.mp4
- **Sound:** Upbeat, positive (tip). Search the mood in the Audio tab, or pick a trending one (↗ arrow) that fits.

Caption (copy):

```
The 10:1 rule for QR codes 📏

Your code should be at least 1/10 as wide as the distance people scan it from.

📇 Business card → 2 cm
📄 Flyer → 3 cm
🍽️ Table tent → 5 cm
🪧 Poster at 1 m → 10 cm
🎪 Banner at 5 m → 50 cm

Treat these as minimums. Go bigger in bad light or if people scan at an angle.
```
