# EndureQR: free growth plan (social, backlinks, AdSense)

Budget: Rs 0. Koi paid tool, ads ya boost nahi.

## Pehle 3 seedhi baatein

1. **AdSense ko backlinks nahi chahiye.** Google AdSense approval in cheezon pe deta hai: original aur kaam ka content,
   saaf navigation, Privacy/About/Contact pages, aur policy violation na ho. EndureQR ke paas ye sab already hai
   (16 guides, 17 generator pages, 8 industry pages, `ads.txt`, AdSense snippet). Backlinks **Google ranking** ke liye
   chahiye, jis se traffic aata hai, aur traffic se AdSense ki kamai.
2. **Instagram/Facebook ke links "nofollow" hote hain.** Yani wo seedha SEO backlink nahi gine jate. Social ka faida:
   log site pe aate hain, brand name Google pe search hota hai, aur naye pages jaldi discover hote hain. Asli backlinks
   neeche wali list (directories, guest posts, outreach) se aayenge.
3. **100% hands-off pe followers dheere badhte hain.** Posting automate ho gayi hai. Lekin comments ka jawab aur dusron ke
   posts pe engagement roz 15 minute haath se karna padega. Follow/unfollow bots, follower khareedna ya "engagement
   pods" mat use karna: Instagram account ki reach band (shadowban) ya account ban kar deta hai.

## 1. AdSense approval checklist

| ✓ | Kaam | Status |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ | Privacy Policy, Terms, About (founder ka naam), Contact | Ban chuke |
| ✅ | `ads.txt` + AdSense code `<head>` mein | Ban chuka (`ca-pub-1520133402245003`) |
| ✅ | 16 original, detailed guides (sources ke saath) | Ban chuke |
| ⬜ | Google Search Console mein sitemap submit, "Pages" report mein pages **Indexed** | Check karo |
| ⬜ | Bing Webmaster Tools mein bhi site add (free, 5 minute) | |
| ⬜ | Har hafte **2 naye guides** jab tak approval na aaye | Neeche topics |
| ⬜ | AdSense mein site "Ready" → **Request review** | Indexing ke baad |

**Agar "Low value content" pe reject ho:** 2–3 hafte mein 6–8 naye guides daalo, aur dubara apply karo. Bar bar jaldi
apply mat karo.
**Kabhi mat karna:** apne ads pe khud click, dosto se click karwana, copy kiya hua content.

**Naye guide topics** (log search karte hain, aur product se jude hain):
QR code for PDF · QR code for Instagram profile (social sharing) · QR code on packaging · QR code for payments (safety) ·
QR code colours that scan · QR code on t-shirts/merch · Bulk QR codes from Excel · QR code for school/attendance ·
QR code for wedding cards · Canva/Photoshop mein QR code lagana · Best free QR code generator (honest comparison).

Mujhe topic bolo, main website ke `src/content/` mein usi format mein guide likh dunga.

## 2. Free backlinks: 30 din ka plan

Rozana 30–45 minute. Har jagah same naam, same description, same logo use karo (consistency Google ke liye signal hai).

**Description (copy-paste):**
> EndureQR is a free QR code generator for links, WiFi, contact cards and 14 more types, with logo, frames and PNG/SVG/PDF downloads. No sign-up, no watermark. Dynamic codes keep working even if you cancel.

### Hafta 1: Profiles aur directories (sab free)
- **Product Hunt** launch (Tuesday–Thursday, Pakistan time dopahar 1 baje = US raat 12:01). Pehle 2 hafte Product Hunt pe active raho, phir launch.
- **AlternativeTo** (QR Code Monkey, QR Code Generator Pro ke "alternative" ke taur pe add karo)
- **SaaSHub**, **Crunchbase** (company profile), **G2** aur **Capterra** (free vendor listing)
- **Indie Hackers** (product page + "I built this" post), **BetaList**, **Uneed**, **Fazier**, **Peerlist**
- GitHub profile README aur LinkedIn company page mein website link

### Hafta 2: Content dusri jagah (canonical ke saath)
- Apne 3–4 guides ka chhota version **Medium**, **dev.to** aur **Hashnode** pe likho, upar "Originally published on endureqr.com" aur link. (Hashnode/dev.to mein canonical URL ka option hai: wo endureqr.com wala guide daalo.)
- **Pinterest** business account: `media/` ki images pin karo, har pin ka link guide pe. QR design aur "small business tips" Pinterest pe bohot chalta hai, aur traffic mahino tak aata hai.
- **Quora** aur **Reddit** (r/smallbusiness, r/restaurateur, r/graphic_design): sawal ka poora jawab do, link sirf jab sach mein kaam ka ho. Spam karoge to ban.

### Hafta 3: Journalist requests (free tiers)
- **Featured.com**, **Qwoted**, **Help a B2B Writer**, **SourceBottle**: journalists experts se quotes maangte hain. "QR codes", "small business marketing", "scams" wale sawal pe 3–4 line ka expert jawab do. Publish hua to bari news/blog site se dofollow link milta hai. Ye sab se qeemti free backlinks hain.

### Hafta 4: Outreach
- Google pe search: `"qr code" "useful resources"`, `"restaurant marketing" "resources"`, `"free tools for small business"`.
  Jo pages tools ki list dete hain, unhe chhoti email: hamara free generator / koi guide un ki list ke liye fit hai.
- **Guest posts**: small-business, restaurant-marketing, event-planning blogs ko ek topic pitch karo (jaise "5 reasons QR codes stop working"). Article ke andar ek link endureqr.com pe.
- Pakistani tech/startup blogs aur communities mein "Lahore se bana free tool" ki kahani. Local story pitch asaan chalti hai.

**Outreach email (template):**
> Subject: A free resource for your [page name] list
>
> Hi [name], I read your [page/article] on [topic]. I run EndureQR, a free QR code generator with no sign-up or watermark, and wrote a guide on [guide title]: [link]. It might be useful for your readers next to [something they already list]. Either way, thanks for the great list.
> Armaghan, EndureQR

Ek sheet rakho: site, date, kis ko bheja, jawab aaya/nahi, link mila/nahi.

## 3. Instagram + Facebook

### Profile (aaj hi set karo)
- **Name field:** `EndureQR · Free QR Code Generator` (name field search mein aata hai)
- **Bio:**
  > Free QR codes that never stop working 🟩
  > 1 tip a day on QR design, printing & safety
  > No sign-up · No watermark
  > 👇 Free generator + guides
- **Link:** `https://endureqr.com/guides` (Linktree ki zaroorat nahi)
- **Highlights:** "Size", "Design", "Safety", "WiFi" (stories ko in mein save karo)
- Day 1, 4 aur 30 wale carousels ko profile pe **pin** kar do

### Content plan (ban chuka, auto-post hoga)
30 din: 10 Reels, 10 carousels, 10 single images. Har post ek guide pe le jata hai. Aakhri slide pe scannable QR code
hai jo seedha guide kholta hai.

### Roz 15 minute (ye automate mat karna)
1. Apni post ke har comment ka jawab pehle 1 ghante mein (reach ke liye sab se zaroori).
2. 5–10 small business accounts (restaurants, cafés, realtors, event planners, designers) ki posts pe sach mein kaam ka comment.
3. 1–2 Facebook groups (small business owners, restaurant owners, graphic designers) mein madadgaar jawab. Har jagah link mat chipkao.

### Muft extra reach
- Wohi Reels (`media/dNN.mp4`) **YouTube Shorts** aur **TikTok** pe bhi daal do. Bilkul free, zyada log.
- Reels abhi bina awaaz ke text slides hain. Haath se upload karte waqt app ki koi trending audio laga doge to reach badhegi.

### Kya umeed rakhein
Ads ke bagair QR jaise niche mein pehle mahine dheere growth normal hai. Sab se zyada faida reels, saves aur shares se
milega. Har hafte Instagram **Insights** dekho: jis type ki post ke saves/shares zyada hon, agle 30 din mein wo zyada banayenge.
